About Us

A Little Gem in Balzan

Cumbo is a cosy cafe and bistro on Balzan's main street, just steps from the parish church. Pop in for your morning coffee and a freshly baked croissant, stay for brunch, or wind down with a spritz in the afternoon.

Sit inside or out on our terrace, and bring your four-legged friend along. Dogs are always welcome at Cumbo.
